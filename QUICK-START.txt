ON YOUR XUBUNTU MACHINE, RUN THESE COMMANDS:

Step 1: Download ISO (5 minutes)
---------------------------------
wget https://dl.webconverger.com/latest.iso -O kiosk.iso

https://webconverger.com/download/


Step 2: Flash to USB (2 minutes)
---------------------------------
# Find your USB drive
lsblk

# Flash (replace sdX with your drive, like sdb or sdc)
sudo dd if=kiosk.iso of=/dev/sdX bs=4M status=progress && sync

---------------------------------------
